## What is the present tense?

We use the **simple present** for habits, facts, and things that are true now.

| Subject | Verb | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I / You / We / They | base form | I **work** from home. |
| He / She / It | verb + **s** | She **works** from home. |

<Tip>
  Use **do / does** in questions: **Do** you walk to work? **Does** she drink tea?
</Tip>

## Try it

Change the sentences so they are true for you.

- I usually ______ every morning.
- I live in ______.
- I work / study at ______.

<Note>
  Remember: **he / she / it** takes **-s** — she **walks**, he **takes**, it **helps**.
</Note>
