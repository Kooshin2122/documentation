Kahor intaadan bilaabin casharrada Forex, waxaa muhiim ah inaad diyaariso qalabka aad ku shaqeyn doonto.

Qaybtan waxaan ku baraneynaa **3 tools oo muhiim ah** oo aan inta badan isticmaali doono inta uu koorsadu socdo.

Waxaan kugula talineynaa inaad dhamaystirto saddexdan setup ka hor intaadan u gudbin casharrada xiga.

## Setup Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="TradingView" icon="chart-line">
    Si aad u daawato una falanqeyso charts-ka.

    TradingView waa platform aan u isticmaali doono **chart analysis**.

    Video-kan waxaad ku baran doontaa sida loo isticmaalo TradingView iyo meelaha ugu muhiimsan ee platform-ka.

    Waxaad baran doontaa:

    * Sida loo raadiyo currency pairs.
    * Sida loo furo charts.
    * Sida loo beddelo timeframes.
    * Sida loo isticmaalo drawing tools.
    * Sida loo isticmaalo indicators.
    * Sida loo diyaariyo chart-kaaga si aad u sameyso analysis.

    <iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/rJj4ZbnzEGs" title="Sida loo isticmaalo TradingView" frameborder="0" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowfullscreen />

    Daawo video-ga kadibna ku celceli TradingView adiga oo isticmaalaya chart-kaaga.

  </Step>
  <Step title="MetaTrader 5 (MT5)" icon="desktop">
    Si aad u barato trading platform-ka iyo sida orders loo isticmaalo.

    MetaTrader 5, oo loo yaqaan **MT5**, waa trading platform aan ku baran doono sida trading orders loo maareeyo.

    Video-kan wuxuu ku tusayaa sida loo soo dejisto, loo rakibo, loona diyaariyo MetaTrader 5.

    Waxaad baran doontaa:

    * Sida loo rakibo MT5.
    * Sida loo fahmo interface-ka.
    * Sida loo raadiyo currency pairs.
    * Sida loo furo charts.
    * Sida loo fahmo Buy & Sell.
    * Meelaha muhiimka ah ee MT5.

    <iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/rJj4ZbnzEGs" title="Sida loo sameysto MetaTrader 5" frameborder="0" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowfullscreen />

    Daawo video-ga oo raac tallaabooyinka si aad MT5-kaaga u diyaariso.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Demo Account" icon="flask">
    Si aad ugu tababarto trading adigoon isticmaalin lacag dhab ah.

    Demo Account waa account tababar ah oo kuu oggolaanaya inaad ku tababarto trading adigoo isticmaalaya **virtual money**, halkii aad isticmaali lahayd lacag dhab ah.

    Video-kan waxaad ku baran doontaa sida loo sameysto loona diyaariyo Demo Account-kaaga.

    Waxaad baran doontaa:

    * Sida loo doorto broker/platform ku habboon.
    * Sida loo sameysto Demo Account.
    * Sida loo doorto balance-ka demo-ga.
    * Sida account-ka loogu xiro MetaTrader 5.
    * Sida loo hubiyo in account-kaagu si sax ah u shaqeynayo.

    <iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/rJj4ZbnzEGs" title="Sida loo furo Demo Account" frameborder="0" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowfullscreen />

    <Warning>
      Demo Account-ka waxaa loogu talagalay practice. Ha u isticmaalin inaad u qaadato in natiijooyinka demo-ga ay si toos ah uga dhigan yihiin natiijooyin marka lacag dhab ah la isticmaalo.
    </Warning>

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Setup complete

Markaad dhamaystirto saddexda video, waxaad yeelan doontaa dhammaan qalabkii aad u baahnayd si aad ugu bilowdo koorsada.

**TradingView** → Chart Analysis
**MetaTrader 5** → Trading Platform
**Demo Account** → Practice

Hadda waxaad diyaar u tahay inaad bilowdo **Forex Fundamentals** oo aan ka bilaabi doono fahamka Forex iyo sida suuqa uu u shaqeeyo.
